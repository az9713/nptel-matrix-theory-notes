# Domain: Matrix Theory

The manuscript develops NPTEL course 111108157 over finite-dimensional real
and complex spaces, for readers who can multiply small matrices but seek proofs
and computational judgment. The ordered lecture spine is in
`lecture-inventory.csv`. Chapter prose is in `chapters/chNN.md` and paired labs
are in `code/chNN.py`. The original examples and labs carry synthetic inputs.

Check algebra, shape, scalar field, indexing and adjoints before reviewing prose.
In particular, check orthogonal complements against the correct ambient space,
norm bounds against the stated vector and matrix norms, and similarity against
unitary similarity and congruence. Test eigenvalue conclusions against repeated
and defective spectra, Rayleigh bounds against reversed order, and inverse
estimates against rank loss. Verify whether a result concerns exact arithmetic
or a specific floating-point procedure. Treat printed figures and values as claims
with independent computations. Check source anchors against the private cue map.

Use the science-editor standard for claims, assumptions, proof steps, limits,
and worked checks. Record findings by chapter and line with evidence and a
feasible repair. Reviews and targeted checks refer to content digests.
